Pumpwise

Pumpwise: a fuel log for iPhone and iPad that gets the math right for gas and diesel cars, EVs and plug-in hybrids. Log fill-ups, charges and costs, see real consumption and cost per mile, and get service reminders. No account and no subscription; your log stays on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account? Where is my log stored?

No account is needed. Pumpwise has no server: your vehicles, records and receipt photos stay on your iPhone or iPad, and nothing is synced between devices.

How do I bring my history from another app?

Export your log as a CSV file in the other app (Fuelly, Fuelio, Drivvo, Simply Auto, Spritmonitor, Road Trip MPG and LubeLogger are recognized; any spreadsheet with dates, odometer readings and amounts works too). Then in Pumpwise open Settings › Import from another app and choose the file. Nothing changes until you confirm, and Undo takes it back.

Why does consumption appear only after the second full fill-up?

Pumpwise uses the full-to-full method: the fuel of every fill-up between two full tanks is divided by the distance between them. That needs two full fill-ups. Partial fill-ups are added to the next full one; if you forgot to log a fill-up, turn on “I missed recording a fill-up before this one” so that gap is skipped instead of guessed.

How do I move my log to a new iPhone?

On the old device: Settings › Backup and export › Save a backup (for example to iCloud Drive). On the new device: install Pumpwise, tap “Restore a backup or import” on the first screen (or Settings › Backup and export › Restore a backup) and choose the file.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete all data removes every record, photo and automatic copy from the device; deleting the app does the same. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay there, so delete them there too.
