Privacy Policy

2026-10-12

Summary

Pumpwise has no account, no server and no analytics. Your vehicles, fill-ups, charges, expenses, reminders and receipt photos stay on your device. We (yusgoktech, the developer) never receive them.

What the app stores, and where

The details you enter (vehicles, odometer readings, fill-ups and charges, prices, expenses, service visits, reminders, notes) and the receipt photos you add are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. The operating system may include this storage in your device backup (such as iCloud), under your own account and that service’s terms.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a receipt or an invoice. When you choose photos from your library, the system picker gives the app only the photos you select. Photos are resized on the device and never uploaded.

Notifications

Service and inspection reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. No notification server is involved, and nothing about your vehicle leaves the device.

Files you import

When you import a file exported from another app, it is read on your device only. Pumpwise does not connect to other apps or services.

Data we collect

None. The app makes no network requests for your data, contains no advertising or tracking code, does not use your location, and does not share anything with third parties.

Purchases

Pumpwise is sold by Apple on the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Backups and exports you make

A backup (ZIP) or a spreadsheet (CSV) is created only when you ask for it, and goes wherever you send it from the share sheet. Keep these files somewhere safe: they contain your records and receipt photos.

Deleting your data

Deleting a record removes it from your log at once. So that Undo and the automatic daily copies keep working, its photo files stay on the device for 15 days and are removed the next time you open the app after that. Settings › Delete all data removes every record, photo and automatic copy immediately. Deleting the app removes everything as well.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on our website with a new date. Questions: yusgoktech@gmail.com.
